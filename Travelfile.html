Hundred Islands National Park

About the Place

Located in Alaminos, Pangasinan — the Hundred Islands National Park is made up of 124 islands at low tide and 123 at high tide.

Popular spots include Governor's Island, Quezon Island, and Children's Island — perfect for swimming, snorkeling, and sightseeing.
